01 / Business problems

Where is work getting stuck?

The useful starting point is a problem your team recognizes. These are some of the places FCAI can help.

01

Information takes too long to pull together

Reports, documents, and systems each hold part of the answer. FCAI connects the relevant sources and builds reporting or search around the decisions people need to make.

02

Routine work keeps filling the week

Preparing documents, moving information between tools, and chasing updates can crowd out more valuable work. FCAI helps simplify those workflows and automate the parts that can be checked reliably.

03

AI experiments have not become a working approach

A few people are trying tools, but the business still needs priorities, data rules, and a way to judge what is worth expanding. FCAI helps turn those experiments into a plan people can act on.

Explore how FCAI can help

02 / Team adoption

Build around the people who will use it

A workflow can be technically sound and still fail in practice. FCAI involves the people doing the work early, tests against real tasks, and makes time for the questions that surface when habits change.

Training, documentation, and clear responsibilities are part of implementation. They give the team a way to use the system, recognize problems, and improve it over time.

03 / A useful engagement

A useful place to start

FCAI works best when there is a business problem worth solving, someone responsible for the result, and time for the people doing the work to take part.

You might need a focused assessment, help implementing a priority workflow, or a partner for a broader program. The first conversation helps establish which approach fits.

04 / Common questions

Before we
get started.

Do we need to know which AI tool we want?

No. Bring the business problem and the tools you already use. FCAI can help decide whether to improve an existing system, buy software, or build an integration.

Can you work with our existing systems and IT team?

FCAI starts with your current tools and brings the relevant people into scoping. Access, integration options, data requirements, and technical limits shape the proposal.

How much does an engagement cost?

Project work is fixed-fee, with an agreed scope. Current project prices range from $5,000 to $55,000. Ongoing advisory and third-party software costs are separate. See How We Work for details. View process and pricing

Can FCAI stay involved after launch?

Yes. Ongoing advisory and support can cover new priorities, workflow improvements, and help as the team or technology changes. Scope, availability, and fees are agreed in advance.
