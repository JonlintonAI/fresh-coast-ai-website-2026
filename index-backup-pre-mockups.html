Common Questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

How much does a typical engagement cost?
Every engagement is fixed-fee — scoped and priced upfront so there are no surprises. Most clients start with an AI Strategy Session ($1,000), and full engagements range from $5,000 to $55,000 depending on your company size and scope. You'll get a specific number after a free consultation — no guessing, no surprises. See how we price engagements →
How long does a typical engagement take?
The Assessment phase takes 2–3 weeks. A full Assess → Build → Own engagement is usually 6–10 weeks. You'll see early wins within the first few weeks, not months. We're not here to run a year-long project.
What if my team is resistant to AI?
That's actually where we do our best work. Resistance usually comes from fear: fear of being replaced, fear of looking stupid, fear of more busywork. We start by listening to those concerns and showing people how AI makes their specific job easier. Skeptics tend to become the biggest advocates once they see their own tedious task disappear. Let's talk about it →
Do we need technical people on our team?
No. We work with teams that have zero technical background all the time. That's actually the whole point. We build AI workflows that non-technical people can own and run. If you can use email, you can use what we build.
What makes you different from other AI consultants?
Three things: we're vendor-neutral (no software to sell), we're Midwest-based (we show up in person), and our engagements are designed to end. Most consultants build dependencies because recurring revenue is their business model. Ours is built on your independence. We succeed when you don't need us anymore.
What industries do you work with?
We've worked across professional services, manufacturing, skilled trades, property management, and operations. The common thread is businesses with 5–250 employees who know they need AI but aren't sure where to start. If your team does knowledge work or manages complex operations, we can probably help.
