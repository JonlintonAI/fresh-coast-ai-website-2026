6. Prioritized Action Plan

Quick Wins (this week)

1. Add missing blog posts to sitemap.xml

how-to-hire-an-ai-consultant.html and ai-consulting-costs-2026.html are not in the sitemap. Google may not be discovering or indexing them.

Impact: HighEffort: 5 minutes

2. Delete or noindex mockup/preview files

8 files (mockup-*.html, homepage-restructure-preview.html, infographic-preview.html) shouldn't be publicly accessible. Either delete them from the deploy or add <meta name="robots" content="noindex">.

Impact: MediumEffort: 15 minutes

3. Update sitemap lastmod dates

Many pages still show lastmod of 2026-02-28 even though they've been updated. Refresh dates after each deploy.

Impact: LowEffort: 10 minutes

4. Improve homepage meta description

Current version is good but missing a CTA. Consider: "Fresh Coast AI helps Midwest small businesses adopt AI confidently. Readiness assessments, workflow implementation, and team training. Book a free consultation."

Impact: MediumEffort: 5 minutes

Strategic Investments (this quarter)

5. Submit site to Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools

If not already done, verify ownership and submit sitemap. This gives you real data on indexation status, crawl errors, and which keywords are driving impressions/clicks. Essential for measuring SEO progress.

Impact: HighEffort: 30 minutesDependency: Google account access

6. Create a dedicated "AI Strategy Session" landing page

Your $1,000 entry point needs its own URL to rank for "AI strategy session" and to serve as a landing page for future ad campaigns. Currently it only exists as a CTA path on the homepage and how-we-work page.

Impact: HighEffort: Half day

7. Publish your first case study

The biggest content gap across the entire competitive landscape. Even a lightweight "before and after" story (anonymized if needed) would outperform competitors. Case studies are the #1 conversion driver for consulting firms.

Impact: HighEffort: Moderate (requires client permission)

8. Write "AI consultant vs AI vendor vs AI agency" comparison post

Commercial-intent keyword with no strong local content. Your vendor-neutral positioning makes you the ideal author. Include a comparison table and a decision framework.

Impact: HighEffort: Half day

9. Build local backlinks

Your domain is new and needs authority signals. Priority targets: Milwaukee business directories (MMAC, BizTimes), Wisconsin Technology Council, guest posts on local business blogs, partnerships with complementary service providers (web agencies, IT firms).

Impact: HighEffort: Ongoing

10. Create an "AI ROI" calculator or framework post

Business owners searching for ROI info are in active decision-making mode. An interactive calculator or detailed framework captures high-intent traffic no local competitor covers.

Impact: MediumEffort: 1-2 days

11. Strengthen internal cross-linking

Link blog posts to relevant service and industry pages (e.g., "AI for Midwest Manufacturers" blog → Manufacturing industry page → Services). This helps Google understand your topical authority structure.

Impact: MediumEffort: 1-2 hours
