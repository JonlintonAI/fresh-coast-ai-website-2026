FAQ

AI Consulting Questions from Wisconsin Businesses

Is there an AI consultant based in Wisconsin?
Yes. Fresh Coast AI is headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. We serve businesses across the state and the broader Midwest. Unlike national or coastal firms, we understand the Wisconsin market, the industries that drive it, and the practical constraints of operating here.
What size business do you work with?
We work with Wisconsin businesses. That's the range where AI can make a meaningful difference but where you probably don't have an internal AI team to figure it out. If you have repetitive workflows, data entry, scheduling, or customer communication, we can likely help.
How do you work with clients outside Milwaukee?
We use a hybrid model. For clients in Madison, Green Bay, Appleton, and other Wisconsin cities, we combine in-person visits for key milestones like kickoff, workshops, and final handoff, with remote working sessions in between. It gives you the value of face-to-face interaction without the overhead of daily on-site consulting.
What makes Fresh Coast AI different from national consulting firms?
Three things: we're local and available in person, we publish fixed pricing so you know exactly what you're paying before we start, and our goal is to make you self-sufficient, not to create a dependency that keeps billing month after month. We also focus on businesses, so our frameworks are built for your scale.
