one clear starting fee.

TL;DR: Fresh Coast AI offers Discovery, Assessment & Architecture for small and midsize businesses at a fixed fee of $4,799. Implementation and ongoing support are scoped and quoted separately. Third-party software and usage charges are separate.

Know the first fee before the first call

If you are deciding whether to bring in help, you should be able to see what the first paid engagement costs. Fresh Coast AI publishes one clear fee for SMB Discovery, Assessment & Architecture so you can consider that decision before scheduling a conversation.

One defined engagement, one fixed fee

The $4,799 engagement covers understanding your business and workflows, assessing opportunities and constraints, and defining the proposed solution architecture. We agree the scope, responsibilities, and schedule before starting.

You get a basis for deciding whether to proceed, what the next phase involves, and which requirements need to be addressed.

Why implementation receives a separate quote

A reporting workflow and an integration across several systems involve different work. Quoting implementation after the requirements are clear lets the proposal describe what you are buying: the workflow, integrations, testing, documentation, training, and support commitments.

The discovery fee does not include implementation, ongoing support, or third-party software and usage charges. Any further work has a separate scope and quote for you to approve.

What transparent pricing should tell you

  • What is included: Named work and deliverables, with responsibilities on both sides.
  • What is separate: Implementation, ongoing operation, and outside software costs.
  • How changes are handled: A clear conversation and approval before additional work starts.
  • What decision comes next: A recommendation and a plan you can evaluate.

A published fee is useful when it describes a specific engagement. For everything beyond it, a clear scope and quote matter more than a broad price range.
