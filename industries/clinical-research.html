Clinical Research

When a visit moves, who else needs to know?

When a visit shifts by an hour, a coordinator may have to find another room and move a staff assignment on the same morning. We build scheduling tools for clinical trial sites so the person making that call can see who is available and what else would change.

Planning staff coverage

Staffing rules workbook

We can turn the rules your scheduler uses into a workbook. It records which roles can cover each task, when two people are needed together, and which exceptions need a supervisor’s decision.

Draft staff assignments

Using your approved visit schedule and staff availability, we can build a tool that drafts a shift assignment sheet. If no available person meets a requirement, the sheet flags the gap for your scheduler.

Capacity across studies

If two studies need the same staff or rooms on Tuesday morning, where does coverage run short? We can build a weekly view by role, room, and time so you can compare options before committing to a schedule.

Managing the day’s changes

Staff and room checks

Before a shift starts, compare the visit schedule with the latest roster and room bookings. Give the shift lead a short list of double bookings, uncovered tasks, and assignments that need confirmation.

Visit rescheduling

When a visit moves, use the dates your study team allows to draft alternatives. Show which room and staff assignments would change with each option. Your coordinator chooses the option and confirms who needs an update.

Shift handoffs and worklists

Prepare a worklist from the approved schedule. The incoming shift can see what changed, which visits still need coverage, and who is following up on each open item.

Try it on a schedule you know

Pick a past shift where someone called out or a visit moved. We can recreate it with fictional names and visits, then compare a draft assignment sheet with the choices your scheduler made. That shows which rules a tool can apply and where the scheduler needs to make a judgment call.

Common questions from research teams

Can we start with a spreadsheet?

Yes. A sample roster, room calendar, and the scheduling rules your team already uses are enough for an initial discussion. We can work with fictional visits and staff names.

Can we use our existing scheduling software?

We can start with a sample export from your scheduling system. If your system accepts imports, we can test a file in the format it expects. Your scheduler reviews the assignments before importing them.

Who approves a proposed schedule?

Your designated scheduler reviews proposed assignments and changes. Your study team sets the visit requirements and makes all decisions about participant care and protocol interpretation.

Does FCAI carry insurance for this work?

Yes. FCAI carries insurance for its clinical research consulting work. We can discuss your site’s coverage requirements during scoping.
