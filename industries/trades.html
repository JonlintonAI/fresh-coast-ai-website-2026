Where AI fits in trades & home services

HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, and lawn care companies. If you're running a crew and spending half your time on phones and paperwork, AI can take a huge load off. Not by replacing your people, but by handling the tasks that keep them from doing their best work.

The challenge is that most AI products are built for tech companies, not trade companies. Your team uses ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro, not Salesforce. Your workflow runs on phone calls and truck rolls, not Slack messages. We build around that reality. Every AI tool we deploy is designed for the person who actually answers the phones, routes the trucks, and closes out the invoices.

After-Hours Call Handling

AI answers the phone when your office is closed, triaging emergencies, booking appointments, and texting confirmations. No more missed calls, no more voicemail purgatory. For seasonal businesses like HVAC, this is especially critical during peak periods when call volume doubles but you can't justify hiring another dispatcher for three months.

Scheduling & Dispatch

AI helps optimize your daily schedule based on tech location, job type, skill match, and travel time. Fewer windshield hours, more billable work. Instead of your dispatcher juggling a whiteboard and three phone calls to figure out who's closest to the next job, the system suggests the best match instantly. Your dispatcher can override it anytime.

Invoicing & Paperwork

Job details flow into invoices automatically. AI drafts estimates from photos or job descriptions, and follow-up on unpaid invoices happens without your office manager chasing people down. Techs close out jobs on their phone, the invoice generates in real time, and payment reminders go out on a schedule. Your office staff stops being a collection agency.

Review & Reputation Management

AI sends review requests after every completed job, monitors your online presence, and drafts professional responses to both positive and negative reviews. More 5-star reviews, less time managing them. For local trades businesses, Google reviews are the single biggest driver of new customer calls, and consistent, fast responses signal that you're a company that cares.

What it looks like in practice

A 30-tech HVAC company outside of Madison was losing an estimated $8,000 per month in missed after-hours calls. Their office manager was the only person who could schedule efficiently because she kept the truck locations and tech specialties in her head. When she was out sick, jobs got double-booked or routed to the wrong technician.

We set up an AI phone agent that answers after-hours calls, triages emergencies versus routine service requests, books appointments directly into their scheduling system, and sends text confirmations to the customer. We also built a dispatch assistant that suggests optimal tech-to-job matches based on location, certification, and current workload. Within the first month, they recovered $6,500 in calls that would have gone to voicemail, and dispatch accuracy improved from 70% to 95%. The office manager now trains new staff on the system she used to run entirely from memory.

Common questions from trades companies

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

It depends on how you want it set up. Some clients prefer full transparency ("You've reached our automated assistant"), while others prefer a smoother experience. Either way, the AI handles triage and booking, and anything complex gets escalated to a human immediately. Your customers get faster service, and emergencies never sit in voicemail.

Does this work with ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber?

Yes. We integrate with the major field service management platforms your industry actually uses. We also work with companies still running on spreadsheets and whiteboards. AI doesn't require a fancy tech stack to deliver results.

What if we only have 5–10 techs?

That's actually where AI delivers the most value. Smaller shops feel every missed call and every scheduling mistake more acutely. A single AI phone agent or dispatch assistant can save a 10-person company 15–20 hours per week in admin time, the equivalent of a part-time office hire.
