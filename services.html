Is This Right for You?

Honest answer: we're not for everyone.

We'd rather tell you upfront than waste your time.

We're a great fit if…

You have real workflows AI could improve and a team ready to learn

We work best with teams big enough to have repeatable processes but small enough to move fast.

Leadership is curious about AI but wants a practical plan, not hype

You've seen the headlines. You want someone to cut through the noise and show you what actually applies.

You want your team to own the capability, not depend on a vendor

Our engagements are designed to end. We build your team's skills so you don't need us long-term.

You value working with someone who shows up and stays until it sticks

We're based in Milwaukee and work with teams wherever they are. In person when it matters, remote when it doesn't.

We're not the right fit if…

You're looking for a vendor to sell and manage an AI platform for you

We're consultants, not a software company. We help you choose and use tools. We don't sell them.

You need a pre-built software product, not consulting

If you're looking for an off-the-shelf AI tool, we can point you in the right direction, but that's not what we build.

You want a 12-month engagement with open-ended billing

Our engagements are scoped, priced, and designed to wrap in weeks, not drag on for months.

You're after a flashy AI demo, not real workflow change

We focus on practical impact, the kind that saves time and changes how your team works day to day.

Not sure where you fall? That's what the first conversation is for.
