01 / Information

Get the information you need to make decisions

The information exists. Someone still has to open five places, reconcile the numbers, and put the answer together. We help connect those pieces so the team can spend more time using the information.

Work can include:

  • Reporting that brings the measures you follow into one view.
  • Connections between business systems, databases, and AI tools.
  • Search across approved documents and knowledge sources.
  • Clear checks for the calculations and summaries people rely on.

We start with the decision you need to make, then work back to the sources and checks needed to support it.

In practice: FCAI connected Stanton & Company's Google Sheets trackers to a leadership digest. Code calculates the KPI figures, AI summarizes priorities, and Stanton's team manages the system.

02 / Workflows

Spend less of the week on repeat

Copying information between systems. Chasing missing details. Rebuilding the same document. We sit with the people doing those tasks, follow the steps, and look for ways to make the work easier.

Possible applications include:

  • Preparing drafts from approved source material.
  • Reducing duplicate entry between tools.
  • Routing requests and gathering missing information.
  • Helping teams create and update process documentation.

We try the workflow on real examples, including the awkward ones. Your team helps judge what is useful, what needs a person to check it, and what still needs work.

03 / Decisions

Make the next AI decision with a clearer head

The demos look good. The harder question is whether a tool fits your business. We help you compare the options against your actual work, budget, data requirements, and ability to maintain it.

That may mean an assessment to find a useful starting point, a comparison of vendors, or a closer look at whether the tools you already pay for can do the job.

You leave with: A recommendation you can explain, the tradeoffs behind it, and a proposed next step with a budget.

Our advice is independent of software sales. We do not resell platforms or collect referral fees for recommending them.

04 / Adoption

Give people room to learn, try, and question

A new tool asks people to change familiar habits. Some will be curious. Others will have good reasons to be cautious. We make space for both and build the learning around work they recognize.

Leadership briefings, team workshops, guided practice, and operating guides help people understand what to use, what to check, and where to ask for help. We work with you on AI use policies that make those expectations clear.

05 / Partnership

Keep improving as the business changes

Once a workflow is in use, new questions come up. People join the team. The business changes. You spot the next thing worth improving. We can keep working with you through those decisions.

Ongoing advisory gives you a regular place to discuss priorities, tool choices, and how adoption is going. Defined support work can cover changes to an existing workflow. We agree the work, availability, and fee together.
