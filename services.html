01 / Information

Get the information you need to make decisions

When an answer requires opening several systems and rebuilding a spreadsheet, good records still leave people waiting. FCAI helps connect the relevant information and make it useful in the flow of work.

Work can include:

  • Reporting that brings agreed measures into one view.
  • Connections between business systems, databases, and AI tools.
  • Search across approved documents and knowledge sources.
  • Clear checks for the calculations and summaries people rely on.

The result to work toward: Less time assembling the picture and a clearer view of what needs attention. Measures and source limitations are agreed for the specific workflow.

In practice: FCAI connected Stanton & Company's Google Sheets trackers to a leadership digest. Code calculates the KPI figures, AI summarizes priorities, and Stanton's team manages the system.

02 / Workflows

Give routine work a better path

FCAI examines how a task moves through the business before deciding what to automate. That includes the inputs, the exceptions, who reviews the result, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Possible applications include:

  • Preparing drafts from approved source material.
  • Reducing duplicate entry between tools.
  • Routing requests and gathering missing information.
  • Helping teams create and update process documentation.

The scope defines which steps can be automated and where people remain responsible. FCAI agrees success and failure criteria with your team, then tests the workflow against them.

03 / Decisions

Choose an approach your business can support

An AI decision affects cost, data access, maintenance, and how people work. FCAI helps compare those tradeoffs before you commit to a platform or a custom build.

An assessment can map current workflows, identify promising use cases, and compare options against your requirements. Vendor evaluation considers how a tool performs on your tasks, where data goes, how access works, and what it will take to operate or change the system.

You receive: Prioritized opportunities, the reasoning behind the recommendation, and a proposed scope and budget for the next step.

FCAI does not resell software or collect referral fees from platform recommendations.

04 / Adoption

Help people use AI well in their actual work

FCAI designs training around the tasks, tools, and concerns of the people involved. Leaders need a way to make decisions. Teams need practice with their own work. Internal owners need to know how the system behaves and how to maintain it.

Work can include leadership briefings, team workshops, guided practice, operating guides, and AI use policies. The scope reflects who needs to learn what and how the business will judge whether adoption is working.

05 / Partnership

Keep improving as the business changes

New priorities, staff changes, and evolving tools create decisions after launch. FCAI can stay involved through ongoing advisory or a defined block of follow-up work.

Advisory can cover priorities, vendor decisions, adoption reviews, and planning the next implementation. Support can cover agreed changes to existing workflows. Each arrangement names the work, availability, and fee.
