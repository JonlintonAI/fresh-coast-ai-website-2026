About

Technology only works when people do

Fresh Coast AI helps businesses turn AI into useful changes in how work gets done. The approach brings together practical implementation, business judgment, and the work of helping people adopt something new.

Based in Milwaukee and working with teams wherever they are, FCAI was founded in September 2025.

Our approach

Start with how the business works

FCAI begins with the task, the people responsible for it, and the result the business needs. That context guides which tools to use, what to build, and how to tell whether the change is helping.

The people closest to the work bring knowledge that a technology demonstration cannot capture. FCAI builds with that knowledge and tests decisions against the realities of day-to-day operations.

Experience

The experience behind FCAI

Founder Jon Linton brings 13 years of experience across international business development, enterprise change management, acquisition integration, and AI adoption.

Before founding FCAI, that work included change management for a Fortune 500 SAP implementation. His experience within a professional services firm includes integrating three acquired firms, developing AI governance, and connecting AI tools directly to databases using Model Context Protocol (MCP).

In that professional services role, AI-assisted process documentation reduced the work from 25 hours to 12 hours per workflow.

Founder note / Why FCAI exists

I have been the person who got the system right and watched it fail anyway. The answer made sense. The people expected to use it had not helped shape it.

Work in Myanmar, enterprise change management, and AI adoption taught me to take that gap seriously. A useful system has to make sense in someone's actual working day. I founded Fresh Coast AI to bring the technology and the human work together.

Firm principles

Practical judgment guides the work

FCAI recommends AI where it serves a real purpose. Sometimes the better answer is to improve an existing process or use a tool you already have.

Recommendations are independent of software sales. FCAI does not resell platforms or collect referral fees for recommending them.

The goal is useful progress that your business can support: a clear reason for the change, people prepared to use it, and responsibilities that remain clear as the work develops.
