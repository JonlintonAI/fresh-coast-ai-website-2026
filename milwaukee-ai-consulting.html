FAQ

Common Questions About AI Consulting in Milwaukee

What does an AI consultant actually do?
We help you figure out where AI makes sense in your business, evaluate whether you're ready, select the right tools, build AI into your actual workflows, and train your team to use it without needing us. Think of it as a bridge between "we should probably do something with AI" and "our team uses AI every day and it saves us 20 hours a week."
How much does AI consulting cost?
Every engagement is fixed-fee, scoped and priced upfront. Most clients start with an AI Strategy Session ($1,000), and full engagements range from $5,000 to $55,000 depending on company size and scope. View our full pricing breakdown →
How long does a typical engagement take?
A strategy session is a single meeting. A readiness assessment typically takes 2–4 weeks. A full implementation, from assessment through training, runs 6–12 weeks depending on scope. We don't do 12-month open-ended engagements. Everything is scoped with a clear end date.
Is my business too small for AI consulting?
We work with businesses If you have repetitive workflows like data entry, scheduling, customer communication, or document processing, AI can likely save you meaningful time regardless of company size. A Strategy Session ($1,000) is a low-risk way to find out.
