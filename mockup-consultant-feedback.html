Your Data, Your Rules

How we handle security and privacy.

AI raises real questions about data, privacy, and control. Here's how we think about it, and what we commit to in every engagement.

Your data stays yours. We never use client data to train models, share it with third parties, or retain it after an engagement ends. Period.
Pilots before rollouts. Every implementation starts contained: small team, limited scope, clear success criteria. We don't touch production systems until you're confident in the approach.
Human-in-the-loop by default. We design AI workflows where people review, approve, and control outputs. No autonomous decisions on sensitive data without human oversight.
Regulated-environment experience. We've built AI governance for firms with compliance, audit, and client confidentiality requirements. We know what "careful" looks like in practice.
Access controls from day one. Every AI tool we implement includes role-based access, audit trails, and clear policies on who can see, use, and modify what.
Plain-English governance docs. You get an AI acceptable use policy, data handling protocols, and vendor oversight guidelines your whole team can actually understand and follow.

Questions about how we'd handle your specific compliance or security requirements? Let's talk about it.
