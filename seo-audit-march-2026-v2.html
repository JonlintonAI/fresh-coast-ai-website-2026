Overall assessment: Strong technical foundation with excellent on-page fundamentals. The site has been through a comprehensive copy consistency audit, and every page now has unique title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, schema markup, and complete image alt text. The internal linking structure is solid, the sitemap is accurate, and the robots.txt explicitly welcomes AI crawlers — a forward-thinking move.

Biggest strength: Rich, differentiated schema markup across every page type (FAQPage, LocalBusiness, Service, HowTo, BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, WebSite, ContactPage). This is significantly better than most sites in this market.

Top 3 priorities that will have the most impact:

  • 3. Backlink acquisition. The site has excellent content depth but likely minimal external links. Even 5–10 quality backlinks from local business directories, partners, or guest posts would significantly boost domain authority signals.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Assessment

Fresh Coast AI is ahead of nearly all local competitors on GEO readiness:

  • llms.txt — Present and well-structured. Provides AI crawlers with a concise site summary.
  • robots.txt AI crawler allowances — Explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, and cohere-ai.
  • FAQPage schema on 26 pages — FAQ structured data is one of the most important signals for AI-generated answers.
  • Rich structured data — Multiple schema types (LocalBusiness, Service, HowTo, BlogPosting) provide AI engines with well-organized, attributable content.
  • Transparent pricing content — AI engines favor specific, factual content they can cite. Published pricing is a strong differentiator.
  • Blog depth — 11 published posts covering the full buyer journey create many citation-eligible passages for generative search results.

GEO recommendation: Continue building the blog with question-focused content that mirrors the types of queries AI engines answer (e.g., "What does an AI consultant do?", "How much does AI consulting cost?", "Is my business ready for AI?"). The existing content is well-positioned to be cited in AI-generated search results.
