How We Work

Know what you are committing to

A useful engagement needs a clear purpose, a defined scope, and people responsible for the result. FCAI establishes those with you before work begins, along with the project fee and schedule.

Talk through a project

Begin with a business problem

The free 30-minute intro call covers what you want to improve, what you have tried, and the constraints that matter. If there is a fit, FCAI proposes the next step. That may be an assessment or a defined implementation when the requirements are already clear.

Understand the work before changing it

FCAI works with leadership and the people doing the task to understand the current process. We identify where information comes from, what slows the work down, and which decisions require human judgment.

The assessment produces prioritized opportunities, relevant risks, and a recommendation for what to do next. Where measurement is feasible, we establish a baseline so the team can judge the result.

Planning range: A readiness assessment takes 2–4 weeks. The proposal sets the schedule for your scope.

Build and test against the work it needs to do

FCAI evaluates the available options and implements the agreed approach with your team. The plan identifies access requirements, integrations, human review, and success and failure criteria.

Testing starts with a contained scope. The team reviews outputs and exceptions before the workflow expands. The implementation includes operating documentation and training for the people responsible for it.

Planning ranges: A proof of concept takes 2–4 weeks; a broader rollout takes 1–3 months. A program combining assessment, implementation, and training may span 3–5 months. These describe different scopes. The project schedule is agreed in the proposal.

Keep the work moving after launch

The work includes a named internal owner, documentation, and practice with the situations the team is likely to encounter. FCAI agrees what support is included at launch and what can be arranged as needs change.

Your team becomes self-supporting and FCAI stays available.

Implementation

Ongoing advisory and support

Agreed priorities, availability, reviews, and follow-up work. Advisory has a separate monthly fee; a defined piece of follow-up work can be scoped as a prepaid support block.

Each authorized phase has a confirmed scope and fee before it begins.

Systems and data

Set the data rules
before granting access.

Bring your IT, security, and compliance requirements into scoping. They help determine which approach is suitable and what needs to be agreed before work with sensitive information can begin.

Access within your rules

Access planning starts with your approved tools, named accounts, equipment requirements, and the permissions needed for the work. Scoping identifies who authorizes access and how it will be reviewed and removed.

Controls matched to the information

Public material, internal documents, confidential business records, and restricted information need different handling. The workflow design identifies its data sources, destinations, and required controls.

Responsibilities for AI output

The design identifies where people need to review or approve an output, what checks can run automatically, and what should happen when a result fails those checks.

Retention and offboarding

The engagement discussion covers where information will be stored, which providers would process it, and any temporary copies needed. Retention, return, deletion, security contacts, and access removal belong in the agreed scope.
