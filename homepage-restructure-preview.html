Common Questions

Everything you'd want to ask.

How much does a typical engagement cost?
You can get started with a $1,000 AI Strategy Session — standalone value even if you go no further. From there, assessments start around $5,000, implementation runs $8,000–$25,000 per phase, and the full journey is $30,000–$55,000.
How long does a typical engagement take?
Assessment phase takes 2–3 weeks. A full Assess → Build → Own engagement is usually 6–10 weeks.
What if my team is resistant to AI?
That's where we do our best work. Resistance comes from fear. We start by listening and showing people how AI makes their specific job easier.
Do we need technical people on our team?
No. We build AI workflows that non-technical people can own and run. If you can use email, you can use what we build.
What makes you different from other AI consultants?
We're vendor-neutral, Midwest-based, and our engagements are designed to end. We succeed when you don't need us anymore.
What industries do you work with?
Professional services, manufacturing, skilled trades, property management, staffing, and operations. The common thread: businesses with 5–250 employees who know they need AI but aren't sure where to start.
