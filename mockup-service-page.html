Questions

About the Strategy Session

What happens if I want to continue after the Strategy Session?
The $1,000 credits toward your full AI Readiness Assessment. You're not starting over — you're building on what we already know about your business.
What if we decide AI isn't right for us right now?
That's a perfectly valid outcome and one we'll tell you honestly. The Opportunity Brief is yours regardless — standalone value you keep whether or not you continue with us.
Do I need to prepare anything?
No formal preparation needed. Just come ready to talk about your business, your team, and where the friction is. We'll guide the conversation from there.
How is this different from the free consultation?
The free consultation is a 30-minute conversation to see if we're a good fit. The Strategy Session is a paid engagement where we do real research on your operations and deliver a written AI Opportunity Brief with specific recommendations.
